Profession conventions

What we change for sales

Quota strip at the top

ARR closed, percentage attainment, and multi-year track record go in a scannable strip above the summary. A hiring manager reads this before anything else — it is your headline number.

Attainment over time

141% FY2019, 128% FY2020, 122% FY2021 — year-by-year tells a story that a single figure cannot. Consistency and trajectory matter as much as a peak year.

Deal size and ACV named

Largest deal closed, average ACV, TCV on enterprise contracts — sized explicitly. Reviewers use these to determine which segment you belong in and which roles you are ready for.

Team built and managed

Headcount hired, ramp time to quota, retention rate. Sales leadership roles live and die on team-building evidence — we surface it explicitly rather than leaving it implied.

Methodology named

MEDDIC, MEDDPICC, Challenger, SPIN, Value Selling — your methodology is a searchable keyword and a signal about how you sell. We name it and show where you applied it.

Marketing: campaign proof

For marketing roles — channel mix, campaign ROI, pipeline generated, MQL-to-SQL conversion. Brand work linked to revenue outcomes wherever possible.
