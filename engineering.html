Profession conventions

What we change for engineering

Metrics on every line

Latency, uptime, throughput, cost saved, users served. Where a bullet carries no number we work with you to find one — p99, percentage improvement, scale handled.

GitHub linked prominently

Your profile goes in the header, not the skills section. Hiring managers open it before reading your summary — it needs to be findable in under five seconds.

Stack stated precisely

Go, not "Golang". Kubernetes, not "container orchestration". ATS parsers match exact strings from job descriptions — we use the terms the role uses.

AI tool fluency

In 2026, proficiency with GitHub Copilot, Cursor, or Claude Code is expected at mid-level and above. We reflect this accurately without overstatement.

Ownership over participation

We rewrite "contributed to" into what you built, owned, or designed. Every bullet describes scope and outcome, not activity.

Portfolio page per project

Your portfolio site links to 2–3 projects with a short case study each — problem, stack choice, architecture decision, result. Not a list of repos.
