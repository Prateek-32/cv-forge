Questions

Before you order

The things people ask most often, answered plainly.

What exactly do I get for ₹99?

A one-page resume rebuilt on the structure your field uses, supplied as a PDF and an editable DOCX, with one revision round included.

Do I need a call or an interview?

No. You fill in the brief and upload whatever you already have — an old resume, rough notes, a profile export. We work only from that material.

How quickly will I get my files?

Resumes, CVs and LinkedIn rewrites: within 24 hours of payment, as a PDF and an editable DOCX. Portfolio sites: live within 3–5 days of payment and receiving your material.

When do I pay?

After we reply. We read your brief and confirm the price and the deadline by email first, and nothing is charged before you agree.

What if something in the draft is wrong?

Tell us — one revision round is included and we correct what we misread. Further rounds are charged.

Do you host my portfolio site?

We build it and put it live on a free hosting account in your name, so you own it and there is no yearly fee to us. It gets a free web address straight away. If you want your own domain (yourname.com), we connect it for ₹349 — you buy the domain in your name and renew it with the registrar directly. We deliberately don't host sites ourselves, so yours never depends on us staying in business.

Will you invent achievements or figures?

Never. If a line has no figure, we flag it so you can supply one. Everything on the page comes from what you sent us.

Can you guarantee me interviews?

No, and anyone who promises that is selling something else. What you get is a document structured the way readers in your field expect, that parses cleanly in applicant tracking systems.

What happens to the files I upload?

Your brief is saved to a private Google Sheet and any upload to a private Google Drive folder, both in our own account — not a third-party form service.

Can I see examples first?

Yes. There are 43 complete sample CVs across 13 professions — browse the samples.

My field is not listed. Can you still help?

Very likely. Choose "Another field" on the brief and describe what you do; we tell you in our reply whether we can take it on.
