Profession conventions

What we change for consulting

Problem · Intervention · Result

Every experience bullet follows the McKinsey format. What was broken, what you did, what it became — in numbers. Reviewers spend 6–30 seconds per resume and score on this structure alone.

Quantified impact only

Revenue impact, cost savings, percentage improvements, client retention. If a bullet carries no number we work with you to find one. Vague responsibility statements are rewritten or removed.

One page, always

McKinsey, BCG, and Bain expect one page regardless of years of experience. We compress without losing substance — every line earns its place.

Academic pedigree clear

University, degree class, and any prizes in the education block. MBB screeners weight institution and GPA heavily at the resume stage.

Sector and capability stated

Your primary sectors (retail, financial services, healthcare) and capabilities (cost transformation, operating model, digital) go in a scannable strip so staffers can match you to engagements instantly.

Leadership demonstrated

Team size managed, presentations to C-suite, client relationship ownership. Consulting promotions depend on leadership signals — we surface them explicitly.

Preparation

What we will ask you for

Have these ready and the first draft moves faster.

  • 01Your current resume in any state
  • 023–5 engagement descriptions — client sector, your role, the outcome
  • 03Any revenue, cost, or efficiency figures you may disclose
  • 04Roles you are targeting — firm names and levels
  • 05Degree, university, and any academic prizes
