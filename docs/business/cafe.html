Our story

A little corner for rainy-day people

Monsoon started with one espresso machine, six tables and a promise: coffee worth the walk, food that tastes like someone cares.

We buy green coffee straight from estates in Chikmagalur and Coorg and roast it every week. Our kitchen bakes sourdough at dawn, makes its own granola and jams, and changes the specials with the season. Stay for one cup or for the whole afternoon — nobody will rush you.
