Questions we get a lot

Do you make eggless cakes?

Yes — every single thing we bake can be made eggless, at no extra cost. Eggless orders are baked in separate batches. Just mention “eggless” in your WhatsApp message.

Which areas do you deliver to?

Viman Nagar, Kalyani Nagar, Kharadi, Wadgaon Sheri, Yerawada, Lohegaon and Koregaon Park. Delivery costs ₹60–₹150 depending on distance.

Pick-up from our Viman Nagar kitchen is always free.

How early should I order?

At least 2 days ahead for most cakes, and 4 days for tiered or theme cakes. For last-minute plans, message us anyway — cookies and brownies are often available the same day.

Can you copy a design I found online?

Send us the picture on WhatsApp. We’ll tell you honestly what we can recreate, suggest tweaks, and share the price before you confirm.

How do I pay?

UPI or cash. Custom cakes need a 50% advance to confirm the date; the rest is paid at pick-up or delivery.
