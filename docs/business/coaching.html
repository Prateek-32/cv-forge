FAQs

Questions parents ask us

Can't find your answer? Call or WhatsApp the office between 10 AM and 7 PM.

WhatsApp the office Call now
Is the demo lecture really free?

Yes. Attend one full lecture of your course at no cost and with no obligation to join. Just book a slot so we keep a seat for you.

Why only 25 students in a batch?

So faculty can check every notebook, know every student's weak topics and answer every doubt in class. When a batch fills up, we open a waitlist rather than squeeze more in.

How will I know how my child is doing?

Every Sunday evening, parents get a WhatsApp update with test marks, attendance and the next week's plan. We also hold a parent–teacher meeting after each term test.

Can fees be paid in instalments?

Yes — up to three instalments for yearly and CA/CS courses. We accept UPI, cards and bank transfer, and give a proper receipt for every payment.

What if a student misses a lecture?

Every lecture is recorded and available on the student app the same evening, and the doubt desk can help them catch up.

Do you provide study material?

Yes. Printed notes, question banks and past papers are included in the fee for every course.
