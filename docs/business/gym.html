FAQs

Good questions

Something else on your mind? Message us on WhatsApp — a coach, not a bot, will answer.

Ask on WhatsApp
Is the free trial really free?

Yes. One full coached session plus any group class in the same week. We won't ask for card details, and there's no joining fee if you sign up later.

I've never been to a gym. Is that okay?

Most of our members started as beginners. Your first session is 1-on-1, and every class has an easier version of each movement.

How does the women-only batch work?

From 7 to 9 AM, Monday to Saturday, the whole floor is reserved for women and coached by our women trainers. Any membership plan includes it.

Can I pause my membership?

Quarterly, half-yearly and annual plans can be frozen for 7, 15 and 30 days respectively — for travel, exams or illness.

Is there parking, showers and lockers?

Free two-wheeler and car parking in the building, separate changing rooms with hot showers, and day-use lockers (bring a lock or borrow one).

Do you help with diet?

Every personal training client gets a simple Indian-food-friendly diet plan. Half-yearly and annual members get a diet consultation included.
