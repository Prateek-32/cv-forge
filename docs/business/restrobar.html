The rooftop

Above the trees, under the sky

Nimbus sits on the ninth floor above Kalyani Nagar, where the city goes gold at six and glitters by eight.

We built it for long evenings: an open-air deck strung with lights for sunset drinks, and a glass-walled lounge for monsoon nights and air-conditioned dinners. Our kitchen cooks global small plates and charcoal grills with a Pune accent, and the bar pours cocktails built around Indian flavours — kokum, jaggery, chai, kala khatta.
