Ask on WhatsApp
Does a root canal hurt?

Honestly, it feels much like a normal filling. We numb the area properly and wait until it has fully worked. Most patients say the toothache before was far worse than the treatment.

You may feel a little tender for a day or two afterwards — a regular painkiller is usually enough.

How long do aligners take?

Most adults finish in 6 to 12 months; simple cases can be as quick as 4 months. You wear them about 22 hours a day and switch to a new set every 1–2 weeks. We show you a 3D preview of the result before you commit.

Do you take insurance?

Yes. We offer cashless treatment with most major health insurers and TPAs where your policy covers dental care, and we help with paperwork for reimbursement claims. For larger treatments there is 0% EMI on credit cards and select apps.

Is it safe to bring my child?

Absolutely — about a third of our patients are children. We recommend a first visit around age 2–3, just to get comfortable. Dr. Rohan takes things slowly and never forces treatment on a frightened child.

What if I have an emergency at night?

Call our emergency line. For severe pain, swelling or a broken tooth, one of our dentists will call you back and, if needed, open the clinic to see you.
