Artist Statement

Painter with 9 years of full-time studio practice, working in acrylic and oil on canvas. Paints the landscapes of South India — backwaters, the Western Ghats in the monsoon, lotus ponds and marigold fields — in thick, directional brushwork, alongside a series of colour-field abstracts. Two solo shows, eleven group shows, 140+ original works in private collections in India and abroad, and 60+ commissions for homes, offices and a heritage hotel. Teaches weekend painting workshops and an online course.
