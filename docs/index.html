Is Fieldcraft a resume builder or a resume writing service?

A writing service. A resume builder or CV maker gives you a template to fill in yourself. We take the resume or notes you already have and rebuild them into the format hiring managers in your field expect. You get a finished, ATS-friendly PDF and an editable Word file within 24 hours of payment.

What is an ATS-friendly resume?

Most employers screen applications with applicant tracking systems (ATS), which read your resume as plain text. An ATS-friendly resume uses one column, standard section headings, real text instead of images, and the skill terms the job description uses. All 17 of our resume and CV templates are built this way.

What is the difference between a resume and a CV?

A resume is a one-page summary used for most private-sector jobs. A CV is the long-form record, with publications, grants, clinical hours or licences, used in academia, medicine, law and many jobs outside India. We write both, and can do both in one order. See 43 sample CVs.

Do I need a portfolio website?

If your work can be shown (design, software, data, writing, film) or you want to stand out for a senior role, a one-page portfolio website gives recruiters one link to everything. Ours start at ₹699, are hosted free in your name and go live within 3–5 days. See 18 portfolio examples in 10 themes.
