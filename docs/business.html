Questions

Common questions

Anything else? Ask us — we reply on WhatsApp.

Do my customers need an app to open the QR menu?

No. Any phone camera opens it, like any QR code. The menu is a web page, so it works on Android and iPhone without installing anything.

What happens to the QR code when our prices change?

Nothing. The QR opens a live page, so we update the menu and the codes already on your tables keep working. You never reprint.

Do I need my own domain name?

No. We can put your menu or site on a free Fieldcraft address, for example fieldcraft.co.in/menu/your-cafe. If you want yourbusiness.com or .in, we help you buy it (you pay the registrar directly, usually a few hundred rupees a year) and connect it.

How long does it take?

A QR menu is usually live within 2–3 days of receiving your menu. A business website usually takes 5–7 days, depending on how quickly we get your photos and details.

Can customers order or book through the site?

Yes, through WhatsApp: a button opens a chat with your number and a ready-made message, such as a table booking or a cake order. Online payments and delivery apps are not part of the site.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how much there is — a 20-dish menu is not a 200-dish menu. Tell us what you need and we send a fixed price before any work starts. Nothing is charged until you agree.
