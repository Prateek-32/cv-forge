Painting the South as it feels in the monsoon — wet, green and moving.

Meera has painted full time since 2017, after two years assisting a senior Chennai painter on murals and a 400-work archive. Her landscapes are built up in thick, directional strokes; her abstracts come from the same colours pared back to fields and a single line. Alongside the studio she runs a limited-edition giclée print line — 900 prints sold — and teaches weekend workshops and an online course, "Painting the Monsoon", with 2,400 enrolments.
