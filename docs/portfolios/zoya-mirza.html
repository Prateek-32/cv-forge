From a balcony table to a studio with two assistants, in five years.

Zoya trained and worked in interior design, styling more than thirty Pune apartments — and began making resin pieces to fill the gaps on her clients' walls. Tidewater Studio followed in 2020. One pour-process reel passed 2.1 million views and brought in 70 orders in a month; the studio now has 38,000 followers, a corporate gifting line, and a partner carpenter for its live-edge river tables — 14 delivered, the largest an 8 ft boardroom table for a Bengaluru office.
