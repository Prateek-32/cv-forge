A graphic and brand designer for D2C, food and hospitality brands.

Tanvi is a Brand Designer at Studio Kaagaz, an independent Mumbai branding studio, where she leads design on 11 identity projects, built the studio's brand-guidelines template in Figma and mentors two design interns. Before that she spent two years as a junior graphic designer at Ninefold Creative, and she runs a freelance practice alongside, covering packaging and book design.
