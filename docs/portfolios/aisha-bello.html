Case study

Replacing paper prescribing

Manchester University NHS Foundation Trust · Trust-wide · 8 months

Bringing in computerised order entry

Starting point

Prescribing across the Trust was on paper, with transcription errors recorded through Datix.

Approach

Aisha led the Trust-wide implementation of a Computerised Physician Order Entry (CPOE) system to replace paper prescribing.

Outcome

Prescription transcription errors fell by 45% over eight months, measured against the pre-implementation Datix baseline.

A clinical pharmacist who cares about patient safety, medicines optimisation and the growth of the pharmacist prescribing role.

Aisha is a Senior Clinical Pharmacist in medicines optimisation at Manchester University NHS Foundation Trust. Her day-to-day work is medicines reconciliation, polypharmacy review and antimicrobial stewardship. She also supervises and signs off competencies for two pre-registration pharmacists, both of whom secured rotational band 6 posts on completion.

Before joining the Trust she was a pharmacist at Lloyds Pharmacy's NHS contract sites in Greater Manchester, running a dispensary of 1,200+ items a day. She trained at the University of Lagos and Manchester Royal Infirmary, and completed an MSc in Clinical Pharmacy with prescribing at the University of Manchester.
