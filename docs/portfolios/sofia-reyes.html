scVI-Reg

Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard · Regev Lab · Postdoctoral work

Regulatory network reconstruction from single-cell RNA-seq, at scale

Question

How to reconstruct gene-regulatory networks from single-cell RNA-seq data in a scalable way.

Approach

scVI-Reg, a variational autoencoder that performs dimensionality reduction and regulatory network inference jointly.

Outcome

Published in Nature Methods (2021) with Sofía as first author; the tool has 3,200+ GitHub stars.

A computational biologist committed to open-source tools and reproducible computational research.

Sofía trained in biology and bioinformatics in Madrid and took her PhD at ETH Zürich before a postdoc at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard. She joined Universidad Politécnica de Madrid in 2018, secured an ERC Starting Grant and a Ramón y Cajal Fellowship in her first three years, and became Associate Professor in 2022. Her collaborations span genomics centres in the US, the UK and Europe.
