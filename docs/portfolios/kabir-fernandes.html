A music producer, composer and sound designer for ad films, independent cinema and his own releases.

Kabir founded Low Tide Sound, an independent audio studio in Mumbai, where he writes and produces music for D2C, fintech, auto and packaged-food brands — typically five to seven days from brief to final mix — and scores independent features and shorts with a pool of 20+ session musicians. He trained in sound recording and sound design at FTII, Pune, and before his own studio spent 18 months producing jingles and ad tracks at Harbour Lane Audio.
