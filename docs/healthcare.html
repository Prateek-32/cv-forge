Profession conventions

What we change for healthcare

Licences and registration first

GMC, NMC, SMC, AHPRA — registration number, status, and renewal date go above the summary. A credentialing reviewer should find them without scrolling.

Patient volume and outcomes

Caseload per day, procedures per year, readmission rates, length of stay reductions. We work with you to find metrics that are both accurate and permissible to disclose.

EHR systems named explicitly

Epic, Cerner, SystemOne, Meditech — spelled out precisely so ATS and recruiter keyword searches hit cleanly.

Procedures and specialities listed

Every procedure you are credentialed to perform goes in its own section, not buried in a job bullet. Recruiters search by procedure code.

Publications and research

Peer-reviewed papers, case reports, conference presentations — formatted in Vancouver or APA as your specialty expects.

Continuous professional development

CPD log, courses, and mandatory training listed chronologically — increasingly required by NHS, JCI, and ACGME credentialing bodies.

Preparation

What we will ask you for

Have these ready and the first draft moves faster.

  • 01Current CV or portfolio in any state
  • 02Registration numbers, licence dates, and ACLS/BLS renewal dates
  • 03Patient volume figures you may disclose
  • 04List of procedures you are credentialed for
  • 05Publications, conference presentations, and QI projects
