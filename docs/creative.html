Profession conventions

What we change for creative

Portfolio URL in the header

Your portfolio domain goes in the header next to your email — not in the skills section. It is the first thing a creative director clicks, and it needs to be impossible to miss.

2–4 case studies, not a gallery

The strongest portfolios in 2026 show 2–4 projects with a short case study each: the problem, your process, and the outcome. A grid of 20 polished screenshots tells a reviewer nothing about how you think.

Business outcomes from design

NPS improvement, conversion lift, task completion rate, time-on-task reduction. We translate design work into the language stakeholders and hiring managers use to justify headcount.

Tools named precisely

Figma, Framer, Principle, Adobe Illustrator — listed in a scannable strip. ATS parsers match these exact strings. "Adobe Creative Suite" is not specific enough.

Awards and recognition

Awwwards, Communication Arts, D&AD, Cannes Lions, Behance curated — positioned prominently. These are the credibility signals creative directors look for first.

Design systems and leadership

If you have built or owned a design system, this goes near the top. It signals seniority and cross-team impact more clearly than any job title.

What we will ask you for
