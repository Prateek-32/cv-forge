Profession conventions

What we change for law

Bar admissions and jurisdiction first

England & Wales, New York, Hong Kong — with admission date. Placed above your summary so a lateral hire recruiter finds it without scrolling.

Matter list with deal values

Each transaction named, sized, and your role stated. £640m acquisition, £1.1bn merger, £2.1bn cross-border deal — the numbers a partner needs to know in 30 seconds.

Practice area stated precisely

Corporate M&A, leveraged finance, disputes, competition — not just "corporate". Recruiters search by practice area; we use the exact terms firms use internally.

Secondments and dual qualifications

Client secondments, in-house rotations, and dual bar admissions are significant differentiators. We position them prominently, not buried in a bullet.

Publications and recognition

Legal 500, Chambers, IFLR Rising Star — listed with year. These signals matter to lateral hire partners and are often overlooked on self-written CVs.

Education in correct order

LLB or conversion, LPC/BPTC, training contract, admission. The sequence must be clear — credentialing reviewers trace your qualification path linearly.
