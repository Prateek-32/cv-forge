What we change for finance

Numbers move up

Deal size, AUM, portfolio scope, cost saved, variance closed. If a line has no figure attached, we go back and find one with you.

A real deal sheet

Transactions listed with role, size and outcome, on the resume or as a second page, written so it clears your confidentiality rules.

Licences stated plainly

Series registrations, CFA level, CPA, FRM — with dates, in a block a compliance reviewer can check without hunting.

Conservative typesetting

This is not the field for a two-column pastel layout. Tight, serious, readable at a glance, and intact after an ATS upload.

Buy-side vs sell-side

Two variants when you are applying to both, because they reward different halves of the same career.

Optional portfolio

Useful if you write research, run a track record, or are moving into a client-facing role. Skip it and pay less.

What you get back

Layout sketches, not finished documents — your real one is built from your own history.

The resume

One page, single column, no graphics that an ATS will drop. Header, a three-line summary carrying your two strongest numbers, then experience with a figure on nearly every bullet.

  • Licences and certifications in their own block
  • Deal or portfolio band pulled out as a strip
  • Education last unless you are within two years of it
  • PDF plus an editable DOCX in the same layout

The long-form CV

For board packs, regulatory filings, expert-witness work and senior internal moves, where the one-pager is not enough and the reader wants the full record.

  • Full transaction and mandate history
  • Committee and board service
  • Publications, research notes and speaking
  • Numbered pages, structured for easy updates

The portfolio site

Optional in finance, and worth it when you publish research, carry a track record you are allowed to show, or want one link at the bottom of every application.

  • Two or three deals written up as short cases
  • Research notes and commentary, hosted properly
  • Your own domain, configured for you
  • Editable after handover, no monthly fee to us

What we'll ask you for

Have these ready and the first draft is faster. Nothing confidential leaves the draft without your sign-off.

  • 01Your current resume, in any state
  • 02Two or three roles you are targeting
  • 03Deal, portfolio or coverage figures you can disclose
  • 04Licences and certifications with dates
  • 05Anything your employer will not let you publish
