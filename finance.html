Field conventions

What we change for finance

Figures move up

Deal size, assets under management, portfolio scope, cost saved, variance closed. Where a line carries no figure, we flag it so you can add one before the final version.

A proper deal sheet

The transactions you list, laid out with role, size and outcome — on the resume or as a second page. You decide what is disclosable; we format it.

Licences stated plainly

Series registrations, CFA level, CPA, FRM — with dates, in a block a compliance reviewer can verify without hunting.

Conservative typesetting

This is not the field for a two-column pastel layout. Tight, serious, legible at a glance and intact after upload.

Buy-side and sell-side

Two variants where you are applying to both, because each rewards a different half of the same career.

Portfolio, if warranted

Worth adding if you publish research or hold a disclosable track record. ₹699, or leave it out.

Deliverables

What you receive

Layout studies, not finished documents — yours is built from the material you send us.

The resume

One page, single column, with no graphic element an applicant tracking system will discard. A header, a three-line summary carrying your two strongest figures, then experience with a number on nearly every line.

  • Licences and certifications in their own block
  • Deal or portfolio band drawn out as a strip
  • Education last, unless you are within two years of it
  • PDF and an editable DOCX in the same layout

The curriculum vitae

For board papers, regulatory filings, expert-witness work and senior internal moves, where one page is insufficient and the reader wants the full record.

  • Complete transaction and mandate history
  • Committee and board service
  • Publications, research notes and speaking
  • Numbered pages, structured for straightforward updating

The portfolio

Optional in finance, and worthwhile when you publish research, hold a disclosable track record, or want a single address at the foot of every application.

  • Two or three transactions written up as short cases
  • Research notes and commentary, properly hosted
  • Your own domain, configured for you
  • Editable after handover, with no recurring fee to us
Preparation

What we will ask you for

Have these to hand and the first draft moves faster. Nothing confidential leaves the draft without your approval.

  • 01Your current resume, in whatever state
  • 02Two or three appointments you are targeting
  • 03Deal, portfolio or coverage figures you may disclose
  • 04Licences and certifications with dates
  • 05Anything your employer will not permit you to publish
