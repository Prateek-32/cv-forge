Profession conventions

What we change for film and performance

Credits formatted as the industry reads them

Title · Role · Format · Director · Year — in that column order. Different disciplines have different conventions: film editing, sound, cinematography, and performance each have their own standard.

IMDB and reel linked prominently

Your IMDB page and reel URL go in the header. Producers and casting directors go there first. We make both impossible to miss and ensure they match the credits on your CV exactly.

Festival selections and awards

Sundance, Venice, Tribeca, BAFTA, Emmy — with year and category. These are your credibility signals and they go near the top, not at the bottom under "other activities".

Format and medium stated

Feature, short, documentary, limited series, commercial, music video — each listed explicitly. Avid vs Premiere vs DaVinci Resolve matters for post-production roles and goes in a tools section.

Union and guild membership

IATSE, ACE, DGA, SAG-AFTRA, Musicians' Union — stated with membership number where appropriate. On union productions this is a prerequisite, not a footnote.

Languages and special skills

For performers — languages, accents, instruments, physical skills. Listed precisely because casting breakdowns search these terms directly.
